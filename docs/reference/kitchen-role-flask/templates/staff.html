Staff & access

Everyone who can sign in, and what they can reach. Staff sign in with their own name and PIN rather than a shared password, so every delivery marked and every note written has a name against it.

What the kitchen role can and cannot do
Can
  • See today’s orders and mark delivered / skipped
  • Write a note against one order
  • Tick off the prep list
  • See the delivery address, phone and map pin
  • See meals remaining, so a renewal gets flagged
  • Open and print the shift sheet
Cannot
  • See any price, revenue or payment — these screens have no money on them at all
  • Create, edit, pause, renew or delete a subscription
  • Change the menu, plans or brochure
  • Reach settings, exports, backups, CRM or invoices
  • Change any day other than today
